Remitarc Desk Tracker: what it sends

The desk tracker is a browser extension for Chrome and Edge. It tells the Remitarc away board whether you are at your computer, so work in other apps counts.

What it sends

Every 30 seconds, and when your state changes, it sends to remitarc.cobsolution.cloud:

  • One state: active, idle (no keyboard or mouse for 5 minutes), locked (screen locked), or paused.
  • The time it was sent and the extension version.
  • A device key that links the computer to your Remitarc account. Remitarc stores only a hash of it.

What it never reads or sends

  • Which sites or tabs you open, or their contents.
  • What you type, screenshots, files, or your location.

Your control

  • You connect it yourself from My day, and you can pause it from the extension icon. The board then shows "Tracker paused".
  • Removing the extension, or disconnecting the computer, stops it.
  • When the browser is fully closed nothing runs, and the board shows you as offline.

Who sees it

Your team lead and Remitarc admins see your current state and daily totals on the away board. Raw signals are kept for 48 hours for troubleshooting.
